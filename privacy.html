WEBSITE INFORMATION PRIVACY

Privacy policy

Sunil Stickering & Digitals respects your privacy. This policy describes information handled when you browse this website or contact the business.

Information you provide

This website does not currently provide a customer account, payment checkout or website enquiry form. When you choose to call, email or message us through WhatsApp, the information you share is handled through that service and by Sunil Stickering & Digitals to respond to your enquiry.

Google Maps and reviews

This website may display location information, Google Maps links and Google user reviews retrieved through Google Maps Platform Places API. Google may collect and process information about your use of Google Maps content and services in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. Google Maps content is subject to the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service. Review text and ratings are user-submitted Google content; reviewer names, profile links and other available attribution are displayed with the review.

Website hosting and administration

The website is hosted through Vercel. The private product administration area uses Supabase authentication, database and image storage. Only approved administrators can sign in and manage catalogue entries. Do not share administrator credentials.

External websites

Links to Google Maps, WhatsApp, email and other third-party services take you to services operated by those providers. Their own privacy policies and terms apply.
